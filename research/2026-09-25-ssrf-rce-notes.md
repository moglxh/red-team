# SSRF and RCE Research Notes

## Topics Studied

- SSRF
- Blind SSRF
- Internal Services
- RCE
- Oracle PeopleSoft
- ShinyHunters

## Key Takeaways

- SSRF can expose internal services.
- Internal trust boundaries are dangerous.
- Vulnerability chaining often increases impact.
- RCE provides significantly greater control than SSRF.
- Enterprise applications are high-value targets.
